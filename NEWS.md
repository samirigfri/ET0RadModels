# ET0RadModels 1.0.0

* Initial release of the radiation-based ET0 models package.
* 10 radiation-based empirical ET0 models (Jensen-Haise, McGuinness-Bordne,
  Irmak, Makkink, Turc, Caprio, Abtew, Priestley-Taylor, Jones-Ritchie,
  Hargreaves 1975).
* FAO Penman-Monteith standard method as reference/base model.
* 9 statistical evaluation metrics (NSE, d, MSE, RMSE, NRMSE, MAE, MBE, r, R-squared).
* Visualization functions: scatter plots, Taylor diagrams, monthly comparison plots, annual bar charts.
* Sample meteorological dataset from a semi-arid region in India (Jhansi).
* Helper functions for solar geometry, vapor pressure, and other intermediate calculations.
